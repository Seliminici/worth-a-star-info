Privacy policy

In short

Worth a Star needs no account and has no servers of its own. Your save game stays on your device. I never get to see it.

The only third party that receives data about you through the app is Google, and only for optional ad videos. Then there is Apple: for backing up your save game to your iCloud, if you agreed to that, and for anonymous crash reports.

Nothing in the game can be bought with real money. If you are 16 or older, ad videos are only shown with your consent. If you are younger, the app doesn't ask for consent and requests no personalized ads; Google is then only contacted once you tap an ad video yourself. Details below.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your save game, settings, high scores and achievements, plus the name and character you pick in the game, and your year of birth.
  • All of this is stored in the app's storage on your device. It is never transferred to me.
  • “Export save” creates a file (.worthastar) that you save or pass on yourself through the iOS share sheet; to import, you open it again in the app. The file stays on your device or wherever you send it yourself. It is not sent to me.
  • If you delete the app and have not switched on the iCloud backup, your save game is deleted too.

Year of birth

In the staff file at the start, the app asks for your year of birth. Only the year, not the day or month.

  • What for: only to set up ads in a way that fits your age (see “Children and teenagers”).
  • Where: your year of birth stays on your device. I don't receive it, and it is not passed on to anyone. It is not backed up to iCloud and is not part of the export file. Google doesn't learn your year of birth, at most an age flag: the signal that you are younger than 16 and, under 13, additionally that ads should be child-directed.
  • Changing it: any time in the settings under “Legal”.

Children and teenagers

Worth a Star is rated 13+ in the App Store. What your age means for ads:

  • You are younger than 16: the app doesn't ask you to consent to ads. By law, you can't give that consent on your own yet. Apple's tracking prompt doesn't appear. If you tap an ad video, the app requests it from Google as non-personalized and with an age flag; if you are younger than 13, the requests are additionally marked as child-directed. Which ads then come is decided by Google under its own rules for these flags (restricted ads only); it may also be that no video is available at that moment. As long as you don't tap an ad video, the app doesn't contact Google.
  • You are 16 or older: the app asks whether you agree to ads. Ad videos are only shown if you agree.
  • In both cases, a video only starts when you tap it yourself. You can play the whole game without any videos.

For parents: the app requires no account, no real name and no email address. There are no purchases with real money and no chat. If you have questions or want something deleted, please write to me.

iCloud backup (Apple)

On first launch, the app asks whether it may also back up your save game to your iCloud. Only if you agree is it backed up there, so you can get it back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

  • The copy is kept in your personal iCloud storage at Apple (in the EU: Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Cork, Ireland). This runs through your Apple account. I have no access to it.
  • Switching it off: any time in the settings under “Save”. The app then stops backing up and deletes its copy from your iCloud.
  • What Apple processes in the course of this is governed by Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Sharing and photos

When you share a result, the app opens the iOS share sheet. You decide there where the image goes. The app doesn't learn anything about it. Saving an image to your photos only happens when you tap it and iOS has asked for your permission. The app never reads your photos.

Apple and the App Store

The app is distributed through the App Store. What Apple processes when you download the app is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. If you allowed sharing with app developers under “Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements” in your iOS settings, Apple may show me anonymous crash reports. They don't reveal who you are.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and funded by ads. There are only optional ad videos in exchange for an in-game reward, in exactly three places:

  • Gem video: a video gives you gems, at most 5 videos a day.
  • “Double profit” in the weekly report.
  • “Swap task” in the daily tasks.

A video only starts when you tap it. No ads appear on their own, there are no full-screen ads and no banners.

How consent works (age 16 and up):

  • The app asks whether you agree to ads through Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform). Agreeing and declining are both right there on the first page.
  • Before you answer: so that the dialog can appear, the app loads it from Google; your IP address is transmitted in the process. Google's consent dialog loads its font from Google servers when it is shown (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com). For technical reasons, Google learns the device's IP address when this happens. This is necessary to be able to ask you at all (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; for the stored answer § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Google's advertising services are only contacted after you have agreed; without consent, only the dialog is loaded.
  • If you agree, Google may store and read information on your device for the videos and use data such as your IP address, device information and, if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier to deliver, personalize and measure ads (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) of the German TDDDG).
  • If you decline, there are no ad videos. The app then loads no ads and sends no data to Google for them. If you tap a video button, the app explains that consent is needed for it. The game stays playable; you just don't get the rewards from videos.
  • Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency) only appears if you agreed in Google's dialog. If you don't allow tracking, Google doesn't receive your device's advertising identifier. Your answer has no effect on the rewards.
  • Google's consent component stores your answer on your device so you aren't asked again every time.
  • Withdrawing consent: any time in the game's settings under “Legal › Change ad consent”. You can change the tracking permission in your iOS settings under “Privacy & Security › Tracking”. Withdrawal applies from then on.

Under 16, there is no consent dialog and no tracking prompt. Google is not contacted when the app starts, only once you tap an ad video yourself. This is what happens then:

  • What the app decides: it only starts Google's ad component when you tap. It requests the video without a consent signal, as non-personalized and with an age flag: under 13 as child-directed with the content rating “General”, from 13 to 15 as “under the age of consent” with the content rating “Teen”.
  • What Google decides: for such requests, Google only serves restricted ads under its own rules. Which data Google's ad component transmits in detail (in any case the IP address and device information) and whether it stores or reads anything on your device is determined by Google, not by the app. I can't control that in detail.

Google as the ad provider

  • The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (service: Google AdMob).
  • Google decides for itself how it uses the data from advertising and is responsible for that itself (an independent controller under the GDPR). I only receive aggregated figures from Google, such as how many videos were watched, and no data about individual people.
  • Transfer to the USA: Google may also process data at Google LLC in the USA. This is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), under which Google LLC is certified.
  • How Google uses the data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Reporting an ad

Google picks the ads, not me. If you see a video that is inappropriate, for example not suitable for your age, write to channoufi@outlook.de. The date, the time and, if possible, a screenshot help. I'll look into it and have the ad blocked at Google if it doesn't fit.

Friend codes and camera

You add friends without an account and without a server: through a QR code or a link that you show or send yourself.

  • The code only contains the player name you chose in the game, the look of your character, a random friend ID of the save game and, in a reply, the recipient’s friend ID. No city, no real-world data, no device identifier, no year of birth.
  • The camera is only used to scan friend codes, and only when you tap “Scan code”. Recognition happens on your device. No pictures are stored or transmitted.
  • “Paste code” only reads the clipboard when you tap the button.
  • If you open a friend link without the app, the page seliminici.github.io/f/ shows the name from the code. It reads the code only in your browser from the part after the “#”, which is never sent to the server; no cookies, no analytics.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The site sets no cookies and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other servers.

Contact by email or phone

If you email or call me, I only use your address or number and your request to reply, and delete them once the matter is settled (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

Overview: purpose, legal basis, recipients, storage period

Save game on the device

  • Purpose: playing and continuing the game.
  • Legal basis: providing the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
  • Recipients: none.
  • Storage period: until you reset the save game or delete the app.

Year of birth

  • Purpose: setting up ads in an age-appropriate way.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in protecting children and teenagers and in complying with the rules on consent (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
  • Recipients: none; the year stays on the device.
  • Storage period: until you change it or delete the app.

iCloud backup

  • Purpose: restoring your save game.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Recipients: Apple as the provider of your iCloud.
  • Storage period: until you switch the backup off or delete the copy in iCloud.

Consent dialog (age 16 and up)

  • Purpose: asking for your consent and recording your answer.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in obtaining the legally required consent and being able to prove it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
  • Recipients: Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (USA), which deliver the dialog and its font (IP address).
  • Storage period: your answer stays on the device until you change it or delete the app. How long Google stores access data is up to Google.

Ad videos, age 16 and up

  • Purpose: delivering, personalizing and measuring ads; funding the free game.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Recipients: Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (USA), plus the advertising partners named in the consent dialog.
  • Storage period: your consent status stays on the device until you change it or delete the app. How long Google stores data is up to Google (see Google's privacy policy).

Ad videos, under 16

  • Purpose: delivering restricted, non-personalized ads with an age flag, and only when you tap a video; funding the free game.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in funding the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Recipients: Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (USA).
  • Storage period: up to Google (see Google's privacy policy).

Friend codes

  • Purpose: bringing friends onto your team.
  • Legal basis: providing the feature that you trigger yourself (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Recipients: only the person you show or send the code to.
  • Storage period: in the other person's save game until they remove you or delete the app.

Crash reports

  • Purpose: finding and fixing bugs.
  • Legal basis: Apple only collects the reports if you allowed it in iOS; I use the anonymous reports based on my legitimate interest in an app that works (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Recipients: Apple; I only see anonymous reports.
  • Storage period: up to Apple.

Contact by email or phone

  • Purpose: answering your request.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR.
  • Recipients: my email provider (Microsoft, Outlook) or my phone provider.
  • Storage period: until the matter is settled, unless the law requires me to keep it longer.

Website

  • Purpose: delivering the site and keeping it secure.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Recipients: GitHub Inc. (USA) as the host.
  • Storage period: server logs according to GitHub's retention periods.

Deleting your data yourself

The app has no account and I run no servers. So I can't link any data from the game to a person, and I hold nothing myself that I could delete or hand over. But it's in your own hands:

  • Delete the app: this removes your save game, your year of birth and your consent status from the device.
  • Delete the iCloud copy: switch off the iCloud backup in the game's settings under “Save” before you delete the app.
  • Withdraw consent: in the game's settings under “Legal › Change ad consent”.
  • Data at Google: you request access and deletion directly from Google. You can block your device's advertising identifier in your iOS settings under “Privacy & Security › Tracking”.

Your rights

You have the following rights towards me (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR):

  • Access to the data I process about you,
  • Rectification of incorrect data,
  • Erasure,
  • Restriction of processing,
  • Data portability,
  • Withdrawal of consent, at any time and with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where I process data on the basis of a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. You can object to advertising aimed directly at you at any time without giving reasons. An email to channoufi@outlook.de is enough.

Since the app transfers no data to me, I usually hold no data about you, unless you have written to me or called me. I'm happy to answer questions.

Complaints: You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The one responsible for me is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, lda.bayern.de.
