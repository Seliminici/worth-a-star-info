Privacy policy

In short

Worth a Star needs no account and never sends your save game anywhere. It stays on your device.

There are exactly two things where third parties are involved: optional ad videos in exchange for coins (Google AdMob) and the one-time purchase of the supporter pack (Apple, processed via RevenueCat). Personalized ads are only shown with your consent. Details below.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your save game, settings, high scores and achievements, plus the name and character you pick in the game.
  • All of this is stored only in the app's storage on your device. It is never transferred, not even to me.
  • If you delete the app, your save game is deleted too.

Sharing and photos

When you share a result, the app opens the iOS share sheet. You decide there where the image goes. The app doesn't learn anything about it. Saving an image to your photos only happens when you tap it and iOS has asked for your permission. The app never reads your photos.

Apple and the App Store

The app is distributed through the App Store. What Apple processes when you download the app or make purchases is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. If you allowed sharing with app developers under “Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements” in your iOS settings, Apple may show me anonymous crash reports. They don't reveal who you are.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

In the shop you can choose to watch short ad videos and get coins for them (at most 3 a day). No ads appear on their own and there are no banners. The videos are provided by Google AdMob, Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

  • Before your first video, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent dialog (User Messaging Platform), and on iOS then also through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Nothing of this is shown when the app starts.
  • If you agree, Google may use your device's advertising identifier and other device information to show and measure relevant (personalized) ads (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • If you decline, you will only see non-personalized ads. You still get the video and the coins. Google then only processes what is technically required to deliver and count an ad and to prevent fraud, such as approximate region (from the IP address), device type, language and diagnostic data of the ad component.
  • Google may also process data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Withdrawing consent: in the game under Settings › Info › “Change ad consent” (shown where the law requires it, e.g. in the EU and some US states) and in your iOS settings under “Privacy & Security › Tracking”.

Purchases (supporter pack)

The supporter pack is a one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Apple; the app never sees payment details. To confirm the purchase and for “Restore purchases”, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA).

  • RevenueCat processes a random app user ID created by the app and Apple's purchase data (product, time, transaction identifier, country or currency). No name, no email address.
  • The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • RevenueCat is based in the USA. According to RevenueCat's data processing addendum, transfers are safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Friend codes and camera

You add friends without an account and without a server: through a QR code or a link that you show or send yourself.

  • The code only contains the player name you chose in the game, the look of your character, a random friend ID of the save game and, in a reply, the recipient’s friend ID. No city, no real-world data, no device identifier.
  • The camera is only used to scan friend codes, and only when you tap “Scan code”. Recognition happens on your device. No pictures are stored or transmitted.
  • “Paste code” only reads the clipboard when you tap the button.
  • If you open a friend link without the app, the page seliminici.github.io/f/ shows the name from the code. It reads the code only in your browser from the part after the “#”, which is never sent to the server; no cookies, no analytics.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The site sets no cookies and loads no fonts, scripts or images from other servers.

Contact by email

If you email me, I only use your address and message to reply and delete them once the matter is settled (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since the app doesn't collect data about you, I usually hold no data about you. I'm happy to answer questions by email.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.
