How to play Worth a Star

Everything you need to get started. In the game, you'll always find the guide in the pause menu too.

Your first week

  1. Tap Play week. A week has seven days.
  2. Each day, the receipt printer prints cards: guests, suppliers, small disasters. Swipe left or right to decide, or tap the bar at the bottom.
  3. At the bottom you see your team: chef, service, helper. If someone is missing, the role stays empty, and your place will feel it.
  4. Now and then a mini-game is waiting. That's when you lend a hand yourself.
  5. At the end comes the weekly bill: sales, wages, rent, ingredients and what's left over.

Tip: You can pause at any time with the button at the top right. Your progress is saved automatically.

The four values

At the top of your shift you see four bars. They show how your place is doing:

  • Reputation: how much guests like to come.
  • Cash: how well the money flows, meaning prices, purchasing and expenses.
  • Team: how your people are doing.
  • Quality: how good the food is.

When a value is strong, you get a bonus, for example more guests or higher spending per guest. If a value drops into the weak zone, a penalty kicks in from the second week on and slowly grows. On their own, all values slowly drift back toward the middle.

Tip: Under each bar, a small line tells you what the value is doing right now, e.g. “+8% spending”.

Dilemmas

Some cards are dilemmas: both answers have pros and cons. A guest complains, a supplier wants a bigger cut, the team would like to leave early.

Small arrows under each answer show you up front which value goes up and which goes down. There's rarely one right choice. Decide based on which value matters more to you right now.

Tip: A good manager softens the impact of dilemmas. The more experienced, the better.

Build your empire

  1. In Shop › Property you'll find new locations, first in your city, later in other cities and countries.
  2. Every spot has its quirks: the train station brings lots of walk-ins, the waterfront is a dream in summer and almost empty in winter, and guests spend more at a prime address.
  3. You always play at one location, where you're the chef on site and get a bonus. All others keep running without you, but earn less than a week you play yourself.
  4. The switch icon on the big card decides where you'll play next week.
  5. On the globe you see all cities. You unlock new countries with licenses.

Tip: A location without a manager and without you is more vulnerable to bad events. Fill the manager role before you move on.

Staff

  • Applications come in regularly. Every person has a rarity (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary), strengths and sometimes a weakness.
  • The longer they work for you, the more experience they gain, from level 1 to 5.
  • Courses improve strengths or soften weaknesses. A weakness never disappears completely. With enough experience, a person can even move up a rarity level.
  • Move and swap: send people to another location. Within the same city it's free.
  • Synergies: some pairs work especially well together. The staff card shows you with whom.

Tip: On the Staff page you can filter by role, rarity and place, so you quickly see where someone is missing.

Facades and rebuilding

  1. In Shop › Facades you see all 36 designs.
  2. There are two ways to unlock one: reach the milestone shown below it, or buy the design with coins. Once unlocked, it stays unlocked for good.
  3. On a location's page, tap Rebuild on the Facade card. Each rebuild costs a tenth of the purchase price and takes a few weeks of construction. Meanwhile your place runs at reduced service.
  4. After that, the facade's perk kicks in, together with its small catch. Some facades only work at full strength in the right spot, the houseboat by the water, for example.

Tip: The rebuild sheet shows roughly how many weeks a rebuild takes to pay off. Strong designs take longer but are worth it in the long run.

Mini-games

Eleven mini-games, each about a minute long: Burger by recipe, Pizza oven, Delivery run, Plate tower, Burger grill, Wholesale market, Bottle bank, Seating plan, Kitchen pass, Dish mountain and Chopping board.

  • During a shift they earn coins. The better you play, the more you get.
  • Before each game a countdown runs, and the start card briefly explains what to do.
  • In the break room you can play every game just for fun. Records and achievements still count.

Tip: In “Burger by recipe” you get about one second per ingredient to memorize. Saying them quietly helps.

Bank and money

  • For big steps you can take out a loan at the bank. The installment is deducted every week.
  • If your balance goes negative, you pay overdraft interest on the amount. If the overdraft gets too big, the bank turns it into an emergency loan.
  • A balance of exactly 0 is no problem.

Tip: Wages rise a little every year. Keep a buffer before you buy your next location.

Tips for starting out

  1. Keep an eye on all four values in the first weeks. A weak value costs more than a strong one earns.
  2. Fill every role before you expand. A missing chef costs more than their wage.
  3. Play the mini-games. Especially early on, their coins make a difference.
  4. A second location pays off once the first one makes a steady profit and has a manager.
  5. Read the arrows on dilemmas. They tell you more than you'd think.
