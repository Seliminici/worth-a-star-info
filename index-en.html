Restaurant game for iPhone

From food stall to empire.

You start with a tiny stall. Week by week you run your shift, decide how to keep guests, team and cash happy, and turn it all into a restaurant empire that reaches around the world.

One week, seven days

How your week works

Every week is a small round: short, relaxed, and still full of real decisions.

Play your shift

The receipt printer spits out cards: guests, suppliers, problems. Swipe left or right. Your team is ready at the bottom.

Solve dilemmas

Sometimes there is no perfect answer. Small arrows show you up front what a choice does to Reputation, Cash, Team and Quality.

Weekly bill

At the end of the week you see what's left over. Use the profit to expand, hire, or open your next location.

Four values hold your place together

When they're strong, business is better. Let one slip and you'll feel it from the second week on. On their own, all values slowly drift back to the middle.

ReputationHow much guests like to come
CashHow well the money flows
TeamHow your people are doing
QualityHow good the food is
12 mini-games

Lend a hand yourself

Every now and then you step into the kitchen yourself. Each mini-game takes just 30 seconds and earns coins. In the break room you can play them all just for fun and chase records.

Burger by recipe
Empire and world

One city isn't enough

Buy new locations in your city, then in other cities and countries. Spin the globe, see where you already are, and pick your next spot: waterfront, old town, train station, student quarter or a prime address.

You always play at one location, where you're the chef on site. The others keep running without you. One tap switches where you'll be next week.

In the city guide you compete with rivals and collect achievements.

Staff

Your team makes the difference

Every applicant is different: with strengths that help you and small weaknesses to keep an eye on. Over time they gain experience, take courses and move up.

Common Uncommon Rare Legendary

Move people between your locations, swap roles and find pairs who work especially well together.

40 facades

Rebuild your place

From food truck to glass dome: unlock facades by reaching milestones or buying them with coins. Then rebuild any location as often as you like.

Every facade comes with its own perk and a small catch. The ice cream parlour booms in summer, the alpine hut in winter, and the houseboat only really feels at home by the water.

And there’s more

Your place, your people

Bring friends onto your team: scan a friend’s QR code and they join your staff.

Wardrobe: dress your own character the way you like, from chef’s jacket to costume.

Place decor freely: put plants, lanterns, benches and chalkboards in front of your place wherever you like, and pick the background color to match.

Sell places: once you have two locations, you can sell one and take your team with you.

Landmarks: every city has a landmark you build up step by step.

Save in iCloud: if you like, the game backs up your progress to your iCloud, so nothing gets lost.

Supporter pack: a one-time 30,000 coins, 6 facades and 2 exclusive costumes. Ads stay voluntary either way: there are only videos you start yourself, never ads that pop up on their own.
